WHO WE ARE

MEET
CATED CLOTHING.

Cated Clothing is a clothing line that aims to provide fashionable, affordable and quality clothing to customers.

The business focuses on offering clothing that can be worn for everyday activities, social events and special occasions.

The organisation aims to grow by reaching more customers and making it easier for people to view products, place orders and contact the business online.
